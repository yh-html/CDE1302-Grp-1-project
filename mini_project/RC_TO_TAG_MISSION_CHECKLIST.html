Critical checks

  1. Confirm launch dot and search bounds with the instructor.
  2. Carry the props-off drone toward the search area: x must increase.

Stop if the local frame does not match the planned axes.

Which program flies the route? QGroundControl configures and monitors PX4. run_tag_mission.py sends the takeoff, sweep, return and landing commands through the ESP32. Do not upload this drawn route as a separate QGroundControl Mission.
